Make your first share

A share is a folder this NAS serves to your other devices. Files in it belong to you.

File sharing (SMB) keeps its own kind of password record, and it cannot be derived from the one you sign in with — so it has to see your password once. It stores only the derived record, never the password itself.

Creating your share… The first share also downloads the file-sharing engine, which can take a few minutes on a fresh machine. Leave this page open.
